Two local shops

Built down the road. Fitted right here.

Gridiron Bumper Corp. builds custom handcrafted bumpers for heavy-duty trucks. The company is locally owned and operated in Fort St. John, and says its vision is protection, harsh-environment toughness, factory airflow and a secure place to mount auxiliary lighting.

THE LAB is a Gridiron dealer and installer. If your truck is not in our store yet, call, text or send us a request and we will order it for you.

Gridiron was incorporated in November 2020 and says its growth has come almost entirely from customer referrals. Founder Jesse Rison has more than 27 years in the automotive industry. Visit Gridiron’s site.

Ordering

Lead time, shipping and warranty.

Bumpers follow Gridiron’s freight rules and warranty.

  • Lead time. Gridiron says in-stock items run about 4 to 5 weeks including delivery, and items not in stock about 5 to 6 weeks of production. These times can shift with their order queue. Ask us for the current time on your truck.
  • Bumpers ship crated by ground freight. We quote shipping and delivery method for your address after you order.
  • Shipping to a business address costs less than to a home. Special services are at the customer’s cost: lift gate, inside, residential or farm delivery, and delivery appointments.
  • Transit times count business days only, and can shift with weather, holidays and border delays.
  • Inspect the crate with the driver before you sign. Note any damage on the delivery receipt and report it within 5 calendar days, with photos of the bumper and the packaging. Keep the packaging, and do not repair anything until the carrier approves.
  • Local customers can pick up at the shop in Fort St. John.
  • Warranty. Gridiron’s limited warranty covers defects in workmanship and material. Report a defect in writing within 30 days. Read Gridiron’s warranty terms before you order.

Full details are in Gridiron’s freight policy. Everything else we sell follows our shipping policy.

Questions

Before you order.

Does it bolt on?

Most Gridiron bumpers bolt on with no drilling, cutting or welding. A few models need one hole drilled to stop the mounts from moving. Hardware and brackets are included, and some mounts re-use factory hardware.

What happens to my factory tow hooks?

On OG bumpers, everything comes off the frame, including the factory tow hooks. The bumper has D-rings you can use as tow points. Street Series re-uses the factory tow hooks and mounts.

Will it fit a modified truck?

Gridiron designs its bumpers to fit stock, unmodified trucks. If your truck is lifted, modified or has aftermarket parts up front, call us and we will check before you order.

Is one bumper interchangeable between years?

No. Each bumper is year, make and model specific. Some year ranges share the same bumper, for example the GMC Sierra HD front bumper across 2015 to 2019 2500/3500. Send us your truck and we will confirm fitment.

How do I clean it?

Soap, water and a stiff bristle brush. If you use a pressure washer, keep it on a wide fan pattern under 1000 psi.

Do you do other brands too?
