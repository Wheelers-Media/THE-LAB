Shipping and delivery.

Bumpers follow Gridiron’s freight rules.

  • Bumpers ship crated by ground freight. We quote shipping and delivery method for your address after you order.
  • Gridiron says current models are in stock and ready to ship. Inquire for accurate shipping times on your truck.
  • Special services are at the customer’s cost: lift gate, inside, residential or farm delivery, and delivery appointments.
  • Transit times count business days only, and can shift with weather, holidays and border delays.
  • Inspect the crate with the driver before you sign. Note any damage on the delivery receipt and report it within 5 calendar days, with photos of the bumper and the packaging.
  • Keep the packaging, and do not repair anything until the carrier approves.
  • Local customers can pick up at the shop in Fort St. John.

Full details are in Gridiron’s freight policy. Everything else we sell follows our shipping policy.
