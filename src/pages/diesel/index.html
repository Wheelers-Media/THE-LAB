Questions

Before you build.

Do I need an ECM swap to tune a late-model truck?

On most late-model trucks we see, no. Many tune through EZ LYNK with no ECM swap. Send us your year, engine and transmission and we will confirm for your truck.

Do you ship, or do I come to the shop?

Both. Parts and tuning ship across Canada and to the US, or we fit them at our shop in Fort St. John. Duties and taxes outside Canada are the buyer’s.

What do you need from me to quote?

Year, engine and transmission, whether you want parts only or the install, your exhaust size (4″ or 5″, with or without a muffler) and where you are. We ask for the VIN when you order.
