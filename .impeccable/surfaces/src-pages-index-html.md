## Scope and mode
Home page (src/pages/index.html), Persuade. Pilot for the site redesign; Boutique, Store and contact follow.

## Audience, job, action
Diesel/truck owners (performance, parts) and everyday vehicle owners (detailing, tint). Job: see in seconds that this shop does their kind of work, then book a service or shop parts, mostly on a phone arriving from Instagram or Facebook. Proof: the real shop photos (hex-lit bay, polished exhaust, dyno laptop, shop at dusk), authorized brands. No invented reviews or claims.

## Direction contract
THESIS: One shop, two bays, lit differently. The page is a night shop floor; refuses the equal icon-card service grid and the dim blacked-out photo hero.
OWN-WORLD: Black ground (#0A0A0B). Boutique bay: ice-white light, gloss black, white type. Parts bay: amber work light (#FF8A1F), graphite panels (#17181A), stencil-condensed caps. Brand blue #0066FF only on primary buttons. Barlow Condensed 800 uppercase headings, Barlow body. Hard 3px light bars, no glows. Service rows with hairlines, not cards. The shop's own hex light fixtures are the one motif.
STORY: Visitor understands this is the one shop for both grit and luxury, picks their bay, and books or shops in one tap. Phone and Text Us stay in reach.
